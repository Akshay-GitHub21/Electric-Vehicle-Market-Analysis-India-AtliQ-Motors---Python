# Electric-Vehicle-Market-Analysis-India-AtliQ-Motors---Python
Python Project - End-to-end data analysis of the Indian EV market using Python, including penetration trends, CAGR, revenue estimation, 2030 projections, and strategic recommendations for AtliQ Motors.

# Top 3 recommendations for AtliQ Motors

### Launch where demand + infrastructure meet — then scale

Priority: start sales rollout in states that combine high EV penetration and charger density (e.g., Karnataka, Maharashtra, Kerala) and immediately form public/private charging partnerships. (Charging data & NITI guidance support this.) 

### Product + Price + Policy playbook

Develop two launch SKUs - Stock Keeping Unit (one distinct vehicle model/variant) : (A) value-priced 2-wheeler (high volume) and (B) 1or 2 4-wheeler variants (higher margin). Combine with a state wise subsidy navigator at dealer level to show net price after incentives. (Studies show cost & incentives strongly influence adoption.) 

### Infrastructure & credibility investments before scale

Commit to (a) a branded charging partnership (public + home installers), (b) a credible sustainability ambassador/partnership (e.g., an environment champion for brand authenticity), and (c) a pilot local manufacturing / CKD (Completely Knocked Down) → local assembly step negotiated with a chosen state for incentives. This reduces buyer risk, improves trust and obtains government buy-in.
